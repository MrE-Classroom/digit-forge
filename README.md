# Digit Forge: The Greatest Product Quest

An interactive, procedural math game designed for Grade 2–5 students to master place-value patterns, multiplication, and addition.

## 🎯 How to Play
1. **Choose a Level:** Select your level from Apprentice up to Grandmaster.
2. **Place Your Cards:** Click on the available number cards to automatically place them into the highest place-value columns.
3. **Forge:** Hit the "Forge Expression!" button to calculate your score.
4. **Reflect:** Look at the mathematically perfect solution, earn stars, and write down your reasoning.

## 🔒 Privacy & Compliance
This game is fully client-side. No student data, personal names, or written responses are transmitted or stored on any external database.
